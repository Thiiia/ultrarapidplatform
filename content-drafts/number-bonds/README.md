# Number Bonds replacement candidates

These are local, unpublished candidates for the eight Supabase-backed Number Bonds songs. Each song has one lesson sidecar for EasySingle, MediumSingle, HardSingle, and ExpertSingle. Source candidates are copied from `Assets/StreamingAssets/Charts/NumberBonds` in the Unity checkout and paired with the corresponding song chart in `Assets/StreamingAssets/Charts`.

Do not treat these files as published revisions. Publish them through the authenticated Platform lesson-save route so it stamps the revision identity, uploads immutable chart and sidecar objects, and checks the hosted Unity capability manifest.

Four source schedules needed a small final-cue retime to meet Platform's 12-second final-interaction tail: GEMINI QUEEN EasySingle and HardSingle, and grudge MediumSingle and ExpertSingle. The adjustment moves the last gem to an earlier playable chart note while keeping its linked Hit, Spin, and Drag sequence intact.
