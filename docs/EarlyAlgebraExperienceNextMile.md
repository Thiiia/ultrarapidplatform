## Product decision

Early Algebra is one mixed foundation. Its authored equations draw from the earlier part of the Unity equation bank. Platform learners do not need a school-year selector or year-specific database taxonomy. Keep *math challenge* (equation structure and step count) separate from *rhythm challenge* (cue density, pad pattern, and timing). Songs and authored encounters provide the main progression; labels such as `warmup`, `core`, and `stretch` can be used for curation without exposing school-year buckets.

Do not add a per-skill mechanic override matrix now. `AlgebraSkTagSceneProfile.asset` has `mechanicOverrides: []`, and `HybridChartWithFallback` already reads chart cues before fallback heuristics. `ApplyConfiguredMechanicOverride` only applies a scene rule when the plan still has `HeuristicDefault` selection; it explicitly ignores conflicts with a stronger source. The existing rule type is a narrow escape hatch. Use it only when a repeated, measured usability problem cannot be corrected in the authored encounter, chart, cue presentation, or a general accessibility setting. Document each exception's affected equation/operation, reason, test fixture, and removal condition. Skill tags are useful for observing learning and choosing a *hint*, not automatically for changing how a song is played.

## Experience to build

1. **A 10–15 second visible learning hook.** The first authored cue remains at or after the existing six-second safety boundary. Once play begins, the learner sees the equation, the target term, the pulse, and the reason that term matters. Give a single “watch, tap, move” rehearsal before scoring. Avoid a text-heavy overlay and preserve the tutorial skip.
2. **A rhythm loop with an understandable math result.** Each cue follows `approach → hit/spin → guided drag → equivalent equation → next step`. The moving term stays visually continuous; the reference equation remains visible and transforms, with no unexplained disappear/reappear. Missed cues expire and the song advances. Offer a concise recovery hint at the next safe musical gap, not a frozen chart.
3. **Feedback with two meanings.** `Perfect/Good/Early/Late/Miss` describes timing; `valid transformation/try the same operation on both sides` describes mathematics. These must have distinct shapes/text/sounds as well as color. A timing miss must not imply a wrong algebraic idea. Make judgement placement and audio offset calibratable; preserve the Unity song clock as authority.
4. **Variety through authored musical phrasing.** Curate the early equation mix so a short song alternates familiar and novel moves. Choose pad chords, spinouts, and drags to fit musical accents and a math purpose. Preserve the existing maximum of two required pads for a new Hit. Do not randomize mechanic type from a skill label mid-song.
5. **A small earned payoff.** After a solved equivalent step, use a short, chart-safe resolve animation and a visible progress mark. The Figma Experience System proposes “earned constellations” for public quick play; prototype that as a reversible visual reward only after semantic completion, with an unobtrusive reduced-motion form. Leave long streak/highway modes as later experiments until the core loop is legible and measured.
6. **Recovery and choice.** At a song boundary, let learners replay a tricky phrase, try another mixed song, or see one worked step. A practice lane may remove timing pressure, but must keep the same equation semantics and must not silently count as the graded rhythm run. Teacher and learner screens should say what was completed and what to try next in plain language.

The [Algebra hitpad flow](https://www.figma.com/design/dP4cwaO9sqZFkEDoZE0Zq3/Ultra-Rapid-Design----Saheeds-dev-messpit?node-id=3308-1346), [tutorial](https://www.figma.com/design/dP4cwaO9sqZFkEDoZE0Zq3/Ultra-Rapid-Design----Saheeds-dev-messpit?node-id=2121-1776), and [Experience System](https://www.figma.com/design/dP4cwaO9sqZFkEDoZE0Zq3/Ultra-Rapid-Design----Saheeds-dev-messpit?node-id=6014-2) are the visual references. ## A richer contract, with clear authority

Keep the two existing entry paths distinct: public allowlisted demo and authenticated receipt launch. Keep receipt version 1 and authenticated completion V2/V3 valid. Add richness in two stages:

**Stage A: use existing V3 evidence.** Build a pure summarizer from V3 `missionSteps` plus the exact published sidecar/revision. Derive counts per *operation or validated content tag*, valid equation transitions, timing judgements, retries, and solved sets. Mark whether a tag came from authored metadata or inference. A teacher-facing “needs practice” insight requires enough repeated observations and should show sample size; a single miss is not mastery evidence. Expose only compact summaries to dashboards. Link them to the registered launch attempt and assignment where applicable.

**Stage B: version a semantic event envelope only for gaps V3 cannot express.** Draft V4 fixtures before C#/TS edits. Candidate fields: `encounterResults[]` (`encounterId`, `equationId`, `mechanic`, `mathOutcome`, `rhythmOutcome`, `attemptCount`, `hintCount`) and `runConditions` (`inputMode`, calibration protocol/offset applied, interrupted/paused flags). Keep the existing aggregate counts and bounded `missionSteps`; reject unknown/oversized records. Let the server derive public learning summaries from the published content, rather than trusting a client-provided “mastery” score. Use the same fixture through Unity serializer, Platform validator/persistence, and Web migration target. The registered `launchAttemptId`, receipt, exact revision, hashes, origin, source, and nonce continue to bind authoritative outcomes.

Separately, allow a small *ephemeral* host event stream for UI: `ready`, `encounter-started`, `judged`, `equation-transition`, `paused`, `completed`, `failed`. Each event carries the current request ID and sequence number. The host may animate a compact progress strip or accessibility narration, but may never advance a cue, declare success, or persist a grade from that stream. Send events on state changes, not each frame. Ignore stale, duplicate, wrong-origin, wrong-revision, or closed-session events.

For the public demo, a future V3 adapter payload may show `learningMoments` and a replay cue, but label them **demo evidence**. A public adapter event is not an authenticated outcome. Either keep its assignment completion explicitly unverified, or change assigned Early Algebra play to use the authenticated launch/receipt path and write assignment progress from the verified outcome. The latter is the pilot target.

## Research basis and limits

- A [large randomized Grade 7 comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37123170/) found better algebra posttests for two interactive algebra games than an active control. This supports testing dynamic transformations as instruction; it does not show that extra rhythm mechanics themselves improve algebra.
- A [randomized study of computer feedback for math problem solving](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27082020/) supports timely, informative feedback. Apply this to the *specific* timing or equivalence error, rather than a generic failure animation.
- [Research on equal-sign instruction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2904558/) and a [balance-model intervention](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7102/10/6/163) make the relational meaning of `=` worth teaching explicitly. The proposed balance animation is an inference from these studies and the product design; validate with learners.
- An [empirical game-learning study of challenge, engagement, and flow](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S074756321530056X) motivates adjusting challenge, but does not justify a hidden per-skill mechanic matrix. Keep math and rhythm difficulty separable and measure both.
- [Unity Web browser compatibility](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/webgl-browsercompatibility.html) includes supported mobile browsers and iframe storage caveats; [Unity Web audio guidance](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/webgl-audio.html) describes browser audio constraints. Test actual iPad/browser/audio conditions. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/) informs visible non-color feedback, target sizes, motion alternatives, and flash limits.

These are design inputs, not proof of learning transfer or of current hosted playability. The next Codex should implement the slices against current heads, preserve parallel sessions, and report code, deployed artifact, and observed learner flow separately.
